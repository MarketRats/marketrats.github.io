01

What Are AI SEO Services?

AI SEO services are an ongoing optimization process designed to increase a business’s visibility in AI-generated answers while also growing website traffic from traditional search engine results. It is a unified approach to visibility across all the places where potential customers now search for information and answers.

Put simply: AI SEO brings together three closely related areas

  1. GEO - strengthening brand visibility, mentions, citations and recommendations in AI systems powered by large language models (LLMs), such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and others.

  2. AEO - improving the visibility and usability of website content in direct-answer formats such as Google AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, People Also Ask, voice search and other answer-based search experiences.

  3. SEO - increasing website visibility and organic traffic from Google, Bing and other search engines.

Results: changes in organic traffic, rankings and visibility, together with growth in brand mentions and citations. Most organic traffic growth still comes from SEO; LLM optimization is about visibility today and market share for the future.

GEO / AEO Don’t Work Without SEO

SEO, GEO and AEO are not three separate disciplines that require three different strategies and three separate workstreams.

Many of the actions overlap. Well-prepared content can help a website rank higher in Google, be used as a direct answer and, at the same time, become an information source for LLMs.

The same applies to website structure, technical health, internal linking, structured data, off-site authority and brand signals.

That is why we manage SEO, GEO and AEO as one integrated AI SEO strategy.

For the client, this means using the budget more efficiently. Instead of duplicating work across different channels, we invest in solutions that can strengthen several areas of visibility at the same time. Market Rats specialists also evaluate and measure where the resources invested at any given time can create the greatest overall impact for the project.

How We Approach AI SEO Optimization

  1. 01

    We Measure AI Visibility

    At the outset, we define the queries that matter most to the business - the prompts potential customers might use when looking for your products, services or solutions.

    20

    As standard, we track 20 target queries across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity, as well as Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews and other relevant AI search environments.

    For this, we use AISearch - AI Brand Visibility Tracker, Google Search Console and generative AI tools.

  2. 02

    We Analyze the Competition

    The main GEO metric we use to evaluate AI SEO performance is Visibility - the overall percentage of brand visibility across the selected queries in the AI models we monitor. We compare the client’s visibility with that of its competitors.

    Our goal is not simply to increase a number. The goal is to grow your share of AI visibility within your category.

    During optimization, we also monitor brand mentions, citations, recommendations, and other signals that help us understand how AI systems interpret your business.

    We analyze prompts and queries your customers may use, the sources in which you or your competitors are mentioned, and the sources where gaining a relevant mention could create an advantage.

  3. 03

    We Adapt the Website for LLMs and Answer Engines

    We adapt website content and structure so that search engines, answer engines and large language models (LLMs) can understand your business, its expertise and its relationship to target topics more clearly.

    For GEO, we strengthen the information that helps AI systems understand your brand and its connection to target topics.

    For AEO, we structure content so that specific answers are easy to find, understand and use in direct-answer formats.

    This includes:

    • adapting content to target prompts and questions;
    • creating clear answers to users’ questions;
    • strengthening factual substantiation and trust signals;
    • optimizing headings, content structure, lists and tables;
    • implementing the necessary structured data;
    • analyzing the sources shown in AI and answer-engine results and strengthening website content accordingly.

    The goal is not to “write an answer for AI.” The goal is to become a source that search and AI systems have good reason to rely on when answering your potential customers’ questions.

  4. 04

    We Strengthen the SEO Foundation

    We carry out keyword and competitor research, plan website structure, strengthen internal linking, optimize priority pages, provide technical SEO recommendations and build website authority.

    Why is this part of AI SEO services?

    Because today, most organic traffic is still generated by Google and other search engines rather than AI channels.

    That is why our goal is not to put the entire budget behind a promise of future AI traffic.

    We grow SEO so the website can attract more targeted traffic and generate business results today.

    At the same time, GEO and AEO strengthen your position in emerging AI and direct-answer search channels for the future.

    These areas overlap: stronger content, better website structure, greater authority and sound technical foundations support both organic search visibility and AI systems’ ability to understand and evaluate your business.

    Traffic now - SEO. A greater share of visibility in LLMs and answer engines - through GEO and AEO.

    Read more about our SEO services: marketrats.lt/en/seo-services

  5. 05

    We Strengthen Signals Beyond Your Website

    We strengthen off-site content, links, brand mentions, and other sources that can help confirm your company’s relationship to a particular topic, product or service.

    The goal is for your expertise online to be supported by more than your own claims.

  6. 06

    We Measure, Test and Continuously Improve the Strategy

    AI search is developing rapidly. Search engine algorithms, AI model behavior, data sources, user search habits, and optimization methods are all changing.

    That is why we do not treat AI SEO as a one-off checklist. We treat it as a strategy that must be continuously refined.

    1. Our AI SEO research team monitors AI model behavior, algorithm changes, prompt results, data-collection tools and emerging tactics. We test them, evaluate them against real data and separate what actually works from short-lived market noise.

    2. We apply proven approaches to client projects and continually adjust the strategy as markets, competitors and technologies evolve.

    3. We also periodically review the AI queries monitored for each project. Once strong visibility has been achieved in some topics, we shift part of the resources and tracked prompts toward new growth opportunities while maintaining oversight of the positions already established.

    We build, test, measure and apply what works in practice.

    Our direction is clear: not to chase AI SEO changes, but to lead - to be among those who understand the changes and adapt early in practice.

Frequently Asked Questions About AI SEO Services

1

Are AI SEO and GEO / AEO the Same Thing?

These terms are often used in closely related ways across the market.

We use AI SEO as the broader term for an overall website visibility strategy that combines SEO, GEO and AEO.

GEO (Generative Engine Optimization), also referred to as LLM optimization, focuses on visibility in generative AI platforms / LLMs such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity and others.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) focuses on making content suitable for direct answers such as Google AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, People Also Ask and voice answers.

SEO, GEO and AEO overlap significantly, so in practice we manage them as one integrated AI SEO strategy.

How Are AI SEO Results Measured?

We evaluate AI SEO performance across several groups of metrics because SEO, GEO and AEO are measured differently.

In short:

  • SEO - whether traffic and business results are growing;
  • GEO - whether AI Visibility, brand mentions and traffic from LLM systems are increasing;
  • AEO - whether visibility in AI Overviews and other direct-answer formats is increasing.

SEO results are measured using traditional organic search metrics: keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversions, visible landing pages, impressions and clicks. We compare performance with previous periods, seasonality and, where relevant, the same period in the previous year.

GEO results are assessed primarily through AI visibility. We select a stable set of business-relevant prompts and periodically check how the brand appears for them in ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Perplexity responses. This produces a Visibility metric that can be tracked over time and compared with competitors. We also use GA4 and other analytics tools to analyze traffic generated by AI systems, although for many projects it still represents a small share of total organic traffic.

AEO results are measured through visibility in direct-answer formats. By tracking target keywords, we assess whether the website appears in Google AI Overviews, Featured Snippets and zero-position answer formats.

For businesses primarily looking for traffic growth over the next 6-12 months, we would generally recommend focusing on SEO services.

For businesses focused on future market share, we would recommend AI SEO services. Combining SEO, GEO and AEO can support growth today while strengthening your position for the future.

Can You Guarantee That ChatGPT Will Recommend Our Company?

No.

AI-generated answers are dynamic and depend on the query, model, information sources, competition and other factors. The same is true of search engine results - and AI answers can be even more volatile.
