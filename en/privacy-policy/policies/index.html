Privacy and cookie policy

28 September 2026

This policy explains what personal data the website www.marketrats.lt processes, why we do it and how you can use your rights.

  • Statistics - only with your consentWe enable Google Analytics only when you allow “Statistics” in the cookie banner.
  • Enquiries - only to replyForm data goes to info@marketrats.lt and is used only to reply to your enquiry.
  • IP address in forms - for 1 minuteIn forms we use it only to limit the number of enquiries and delete it after 1 minute.
  • We do not sell dataNor do we pass it to anyone for advertising.

02

What data we collect and why

Enquiry forms

When you fill in an enquiry form, we receive:

  • your name (required);
  • company name (optional);
  • phone number (required);
  • e-mail address (required);
  • website address (required);
  • your planned SEO / AI SEO budget, one of the given ranges (required);
  • a message (optional).

The form it was sent from and the time of sending are passed on with the enquiry automatically. We do not include your IP address in the enquiry.

We use this data only to reply to your enquiry: to contact you, assess your website and make an offer. The enquiry is sent by e-mail to our mailbox info@marketrats.lt. If the e-mail cannot be sent, the enquiry is written to the website server's log so that it is not lost.

Some Lithuanian service pages also have a short enquiry window. It collects the chosen service, name (required), e-mail address (required), phone number (optional) and a website or message (optional). We use and handle this data in the same way as the form data.

Protection against spam and abuse

To prevent abuse of the forms, we limit the number of enquiries by IP address: we accept no more than 5 enquiries per minute from one address. We use the IP address only for this count: it is not included in the enquiry, not sent by e-mail and not written to the server log. The forms also have a hidden field that only automated programs fill in. Such enquiries are neither sent nor stored.

Browsing the website

The website has no advertising or marketing tools, chat windows or other third-party scripts, except Google Analytics, which we enable only with your consent (see below). Fonts and images are served from our own website. Videos and the map are not embedded in the pages; they are given as links only. The website server automatically records technical access data (IP address, time, the page opened, browser type) to keep the website working and secure. Form submissions are not written to this log.

Visit statistics (Google Analytics)

If you allow “Statistics” in the cookie banner, we enable Google Analytics 4. It collects which pages you open and how long you stay on them, where you came from (e.g. from search), the device and browser type, the approximate location (country, city) and a few actions on the website: a click on our phone number or e-mail address, copying them, going to the contacts page and a successfully sent enquiry (without its content). Google Analytics also records by itself scrolling to the bottom of a page, clicks on links to other websites (the link address), file downloads and the start or submission of a form (without what was typed). This shows us which pages are useful and how clients find us.

Google Analytics does not store your IP address. Advertising and marketing features are switched off, and we do not use the data to build a profile of you. Until you give consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and no data is sent to Google.

We do not carry out profiling or make automated decisions.

03

On what basis we process data

  • Enquiries: Article 6(1)(b) of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), as we take steps at your request before entering into a contract. When you send an enquiry on behalf of a company, we rely on Article 6(1)(f): our legitimate interest in replying to business enquiries.
  • An enquiry written to the server log when the e-mail cannot be sent: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in not losing your enquiry and replying to it.
  • IP address for limiting enquiries and technical logs: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in keeping the website secure and protecting it from spam.
  • Necessary entries in the browser (see “Cookies and entries in your browser”): they need no consent, as they are required for a function you chose yourself. Their content is not sent to us.
  • Visit statistics (Google Analytics): your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR and Article 73(4) of the Lithuanian Law on Electronic Communications). You can withdraw consent at any time. The website has no marketing tools.

04

How long we keep data

  • Enquiries in the mailbox: no longer than 6 months from the last correspondence if you do not become our client. If you become a client, we keep the correspondence for as long as the contract is in force and after it ends for as long as the law requires.
  • An enquiry in the server log if the e-mail could not be sent: no longer than 30 days; during that time we forward the enquiry to the mailbox and handle it further as an e-mail enquiry. When the e-mail is sent successfully, the log keeps only a note of which form the enquiry came from and the time, without your data.
  • IP address for limiting enquiries: kept only in the server's working memory and deleted 1 minute after the last enquiry.
  • Technical server access logs: 14 days.
  • Google Analytics data: no longer than 14 months. Cookie lifetimes are in the table under “Cookies and entries in your browser”.
  • Entries in your browser: until you delete them (see “Cookies and entries in your browser”).

05

Who receives your data

We do not sell data and do not pass it to anyone for advertising. Data is processed only by service providers that help the website work:

  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365): the mailbox info@marketrats.lt is kept on its servers, and e-mails from the website forms are sent through it;
  • UAB „Interneto vizija“ (IV.lt, Lithuania) - the website hosting provider;
  • Google Ireland Limited (Google Analytics) - only if you allowed “Statistics”.

If a service provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, data is transferred only with the safeguards provided for in the GDPR. Google Analytics data may be transferred to Google LLC (USA), which participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; in other cases the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission apply. We may disclose data to public authorities when the law requires it.

Links to other websites

The website has links to other websites, for example to our office location on Google Maps, to videos, to reviews on Clutch and Rekvizitai.lt and to the client account at services.marketrats.lt. Their content is not loaded into our pages. A website opens only when you click the link, and then that website's privacy rules apply.

06

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • know whether we process your data and receive a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete data;
  • ask us to restrict processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive the data you provided in a common machine-readable format and ask us to transfer it to another controller;
  • withdraw consent at any time where we process data on the basis of consent.

How to use your rights: write to info@marketrats.lt or send a letter to Ateities g. 25B, Vilnius, 06326, Lithuania. We will reply within one month at the latest. So that we do not disclose data to another person, we may ask you to confirm your identity, for example by writing from the same e-mail address you gave in the form.

If you believe your data is being processed improperly, you can lodge a complaint with the State Data Protection Inspectorate of Lithuania (vdai.lrv.lt (atsidaro naujame skirtuke) (opens in a new tab)). We suggest contacting us first, as we can solve the question faster that way.
