What Is SEO?

SEO (search engine optimisation) is the process of improving the rankings of specific website pages for relevant keywords in organic search engine results (SERPs). The more relevant keywords your website ranks highly for, the more interested visitors it can attract - and the greater the potential for conversions.

Our SEO work begins with an assessment of your current position. We conduct keyword research and analyse search demand, competitor activity, your website’s condition and its growth potential. These insights inform an SEO strategy with clear goals, priorities and an action plan.

1

When can I expect to see the first results?

Typically, the first positive results can be expected within 3-6 months, although every project is different. If your website is technically sound, has established authority and features suitable content, results may appear sooner - for example, within two months.

Google itself emphasises that SEO is a gradual, long-term process. How quickly results appear depends on several factors:

  • Your niche and its level of competition.
  • Available resources, including budget and time.
  • Your website’s technical condition.
  • The amount of existing content.
  • The quantity and quality of existing backlinks.
  • How quickly the client, developers and writers complete their assigned SEO tasks.

We do not make unrealistic promises or offer unrealistic timelines. We assess performance indicators, work with facts and provide realistic forecasts. We cannot guarantee a specific date or ranking; doing so would be unethical and inconsistent with Google’s guidelines.

2

What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads?

SEO (search engine optimisation) and Google Ads (paid advertising) are two different but complementary digital marketing approaches.

  • SEO aims to generate long-term organic traffic through higher rankings in Google’s unpaid search results. Visibility can continue after SEO work and spending stop, until competitors outperform your website.
  • Google Ads provides immediate visibility, which lasts while you continue paying for advertising.

Each approach has its strengths and limitations. Using both together is often the most effective way to combine immediate results with long-term growth. We help clients choose the right mix based on their budget, goals and competitive environment. Where needed, we can recommend trusted Google Ads partners.

Are SEO services suitable for every business?

SEO services are suitable for businesses whose products, services or information people search for online. SEO primarily responds to existing demand. If you are unsure whether there is online demand for what you offer, we can help you find out. SEO begins with keyword research, which allows us to assess that demand.

Search demand for relevant keywords is the most important factor in determining whether SEO is suitable for your business.

We also assess search volumes, competition, your target audience and the resources available.

In some cases, SEO may play a supporting role within a broader marketing strategy.

Where demand exists, SEO can be effective for a wide range of businesses:

Can you optimise a website for AI?

Yes. Clients increasingly ask how their businesses can be cited, mentioned or recommended in AI-generated answers. As AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google SGE draw on principles similar to those used by search engines, we have developed a dedicated service: AI SEO, also known as GEO or AEO.

AI crawlers currently use websites to retrieve information rather than assign traditional search rankings. We therefore cannot promise specific AI positions. AI SEO focuses on making your content easy to understand and ensuring it provides valuable, relevant and reliable information.

Many AI optimisation activities overlap with established SEO processes, making the service easy to integrate into a traditional SEO strategy. This helps reduce costs and improve the return on individual activities.

In 2025, traffic from AI models accounted for approximately 0.5% of total traffic across our projects, with an upward trend observed from the start of the year. We therefore view AI SEO as a long-term investment for businesses seeking to establish a presence in AI-driven search while competition remains limited and to increase brand visibility and awareness.

How does optimising for Google differ from optimising for AI systems?

Google SEO focuses on search rankings influenced by hundreds of signals, including page content, links and user experience.

AI systems such as ChatGPT and Gemini summarise information from multiple sources rather than presenting traditional search rankings. Clear, accurate and easily understood content is therefore essential.

We understand both approaches and optimise for traditional search engines and AI assistants, following publicly available standards and guidance, including Schema.org and OpenAI guidelines.

What happens if I stop investing in SEO?

If you stop investing in SEO while competitors continue working on theirs, your results may gradually weaken. Rankings and traffic can decline, particularly in competitive niches.

We have worked with projects where SEO had been neglected and restoring visibility later required substantially more resources.

SEO is a long-term process involving content updates, technical maintenance and monitoring the competitive environment.

We follow Google’s guidance, which emphasises the importance of ongoing website maintenance and improvement.

We recommend having a plan before stopping SEO. We can help you understand the likely consequences and consider alternatives.

However, if your sector and location have few or no competitors, and those competitors invest little or nothing in SEO, your results may remain relatively stable for longer after you stop investing.
