What tasks are included in SEO services?

Whether optimizing a website for search engines or artificial intelligence (AI) tools, the SEO process can be broken down into three main parts: on-page, off-page, and technical SEO. Additionally, to achieve long-term results, it is important to include continuous website improvement in response to changes in results. Learn more about what each of these parts entails:

On-Page SEO

These are tasks related to the website’s structure, pages, and content:

  • Optimization of website navigation and structure;
  • Keyword analysis and integration into the website;
  • Analysis of search queries;
  • Optimization of meta tags and headings;
  • Preparation of content tailored for search engines and artificial intelligence;
  • Development of internal linking structures;
  • Optimization of visual content (photos, videos, etc.).

Technical SEO

Technical SEO ensures that search engines and AI bots can properly read and understand the website. Some of the most important components of technical SEO:

  • Website speed optimization;
  • Mobile optimization to ensure the website is user-friendly on any device;
  • Clean URL structure;
  • Correct implementation of canonical tags;
  • Implementation of sitemap, robots.txt, and llms.txt;
  • Resolving 404 errors and redirect issues;
  • Schema markup;
  • Other.

Off-Page SEO

  • We develop a strategy to increase the website’s authority and credibility;
  • We promote partnerships, collaboration, and content distribution to build a backlink network and secure mentions;
  • We monitor the backlink profile to ensure it is diverse, organic, and high-quality.

Continuous website improvement, reports, and consultations

SEO is not a one-time task. As a project develops, competitors evolve, user needs change, and new relevant topics emerge. Adaptation is also required to ever-changing user search habits, tools, and evolving search engines: search engine updates, the emergence and improvement of AI tools, changes in algorithms, etc.

Our goal is not only to achieve TOP 3 or TOP 1 rankings, but also to ensure continuous project development potential and quality improvement. During the service provision, monthly performance reports are provided, we communicate with clients about ongoing processes, and offer consultations on all related matters.

How do one-time SEO tasks differ from ongoing ones?

One-time SEO tasks:

include internal website optimization, cleaning up the site, and preparing the system for future growth. In rare cases where competition is minimal, this may be sufficient to achieve results. However, one-time services are typically used when building or updating a website to ensure the system is immediately optimized for SEO, allowing for continued project growth and ongoing maintenance. After a one-time SEO optimization, you can expect an increase in the number of keywords the website appears for, but this does not guarantee high rankings - external optimization is also required.

Ongoing SEO Maintenance:

a continuous process of optimization and website maintenance where we operate according to a defined strategy, strive to achieve set goals, and adjust them along the way based on ongoing changes, aiming for long-term results and growth.

FAQ (SEO Service Pricing)

1

Why do SEO pricing structures vary among agencies?

Pricing varies among SEO agencies because the SEO services they offer and the tactics they use to deliver them differ. Agencies use different tools and technologies to collect data and build backlink profiles using different strategies. Some follow clearly defined plans and work with a limited number of keywords, while others (like Market Rats) take a broader approach, working on the project in its entirety and aiming for long-term growth. Each agency can offer different strategies and services, so it’s important to choose the one that best aligns with your goals.

2

Can cheap SEO be harmful?

SEO services priced significantly below the market average may result in poor-quality content and link-building practices that can harm SEO results. Additionally, a low service price means that little time is allocated to the project, during which the goal is to quickly boost rankings using methods based on non-transparent tactics that can trigger Google penalties, temporary results, and a drop in rankings.

3

How long does it take for SEO to pay off?

It can take anywhere from a few months to a year or even several years for SEO to pay off, depending on the level of competition and the resources allocated. In some cases, achieving profitable SEO results may not be feasible. When such cases arise, Market Rats specialists inform clients in advance.

4

Is a one-time SEO project sufficient, or is ongoing maintenance necessary?

To achieve long-term results, ongoing SEO maintenance is essential. One-time efforts can improve a website’s visibility, but only continuous optimization will ensure long-term growth.

5

Why is SEO better than Google Ads or Facebook Ads?

SEO is a long-term investment that provides lasting benefits compared to other advertising channels, such as Google Ads or Facebook Ads. SEO allows you to achieve high rankings in search engines and attract organic traffic, which can be cheaper than paid advertising in the long run, and results can continue even after SEO services stop (as long as competitors don’t overtake you or the project isn’t affected by updates).
